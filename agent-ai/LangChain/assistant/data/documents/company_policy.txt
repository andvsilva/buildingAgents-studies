Company General Policy Document

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Work Standards
All employees must follow internal compliance guidelines and data privacy regulations.

2. Customer Satisfaction
Customer satisfaction is the company’s top priority. All customer complaints must be addressed within 48 hours.

3. Refund Policy Reference
Refunds are governed by the official Refund Policy document. Refund approval must comply with revenue impact considerations.

4. Data Usage
All company sales data must be stored securely in PostgreSQL systems. Access is role-based and audited.

5. Confidential Information
Strategic documents and financial projections are confidential.
