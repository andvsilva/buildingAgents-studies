Official Refund Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2025

Customers may request refunds within 30 days of purchase.

Refund Conditions:
- Product must not show physical damage.
- Digital products are non-refundable after activation.
- Refunds above $10,000 require finance department approval.

Business Impact Note:
Refund policy changes may affect short-term revenue but improve long-term retention.
